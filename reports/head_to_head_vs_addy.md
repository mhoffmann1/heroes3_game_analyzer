# Head-to-head Heroes III analysis: Red vs Blue

Eligible games: **19**. Reliably inferred surviving sides: **6**. Unknown outcomes: **13**.

Source directories: `Addy86` and `addy1986`. A game is included only when its directory contains at least 54 numbered `.GM2` saves and `311.GM2`. The strongest hero is selected by calculated army strength. The final two numbered saves are compared first. If exactly one penultimate strongest hero disappears from the final numbered autosave, the penultimate save is treated as pre-battle. Otherwise, the final numbered save is compared with the first later `.GM1`–`.GM8` save.

Embedded player-name ordering is not used because it does not reliably map names to player colors. Results therefore identify only the surviving color and hero.

Battle dates without a post-battle save are estimates based on the final numbered save timestamp. A strength disparity of at least 3:1 is flagged, but disparity alone does not determine the surviving side. `A/D/P/K` means Attack/Defense/Spell Power/Knowledge.
